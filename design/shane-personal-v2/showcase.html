01 · color

Paper, ink, one accent. Plus one mark with a single job.

Annotation red is the only v3 addition. It marks what a drawing calls out and nothing else. If you cannot point at what it annotates, it does not belong on the page.

surface #fbfaf9 The committed warm cream. The page never sits on white.
ink #0e1116 Near-black with a blue cast. 17:1 on surface.
accent #556b2f Olive. Links, interaction states, and one accented word per surface.
mark #c0392b Annotation red, new in v3. Dimensions, callouts, hatch. Never a heading, never a link.
ink soft #404550 Secondary ink. Standfirsts and supporting body.
ink 3 #62686f Metadata ink. Folios, datelines, captions.

surface feature #ede4d2 Reserved. Still not used as a background. The feature treatment is the inversion below.
the feature treatment

Ink block, cream type. This is what the contact CTA is built from. Inside an inversion, links take the cream text color rather than olive, because olive on ink measures 3.2:1 and that is large-text only.

02 · type

Two families do the work. Mono is technical only.

Fraunces carries the whole display range and the small-caps labels. IBM Plex Sans carries lead, body, and meta, matching what the site has always shipped. JetBrains Mono is restricted to datelines, code, folios, title-block values, and text inside drawings.

04 · figure

Drawn at 1px, so the figure and the page share one line.

A record chart needs a file it reconciles to, named in the caption. This one reconciles to articles-list.json and every number in it is checkable against that file. Hatch marks the region under discussion, witness marks terminate the dimension, and the callouts key to the numbered list beside the drawing. Three callouts is the ceiling.

  1. 12013 sets the ceiling at 12 posts, more than the next four years put together.
  2. 2The run resumes in 2026 at 9 posts, which is already the second heaviest year on the chart.
  3. ruleThe hatched rect carries .s-edge. Using .s-ink here declares fill: none and CSS beats the presentation attribute, which erases the hatch silently.

06 · architectures

Five shapes, chosen by an ordered test.

Ask the five questions in order and stop at the first yes. The earlier question wins because it describes the reader's job rather than the author's intent. One page, one architecture. They do not nest, and a page that blends two is a page whose content type has not been decided. Miniatures below run at reduced scale, and this is a specimen sheet rather than a site page, so it carries more drawings than the density table would allow anywhere real.

07 · absent on purpose

The cuts are part of the system.

Zone references and edge rulers were built for the Sheet architecture and removed. They looked systematic and addressed nothing, because no content ever cited a zone. Filled buttons are gone, and so are shadows, card lifts, and any radius past hairline outside avatars and the callout bubble. A page may carry zero figures, and three of the mapped page types do.
