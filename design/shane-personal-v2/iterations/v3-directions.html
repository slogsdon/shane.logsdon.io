Five directions on the v2 system

Every direction below keeps the v2 spine unchanged — cream #fbfaf9, ink #0e1116, olive #556b2f, Fraunces + IBM Plex + JetBrains Mono, zero radius, 1px rules, no filled buttons. What changes in each is one structural commitment, not a skin. The shared content is real: the same headline, the same five pieces, the same practice line, rendered five ways so the difference is attributable to the system and not the copy.

A / Ledger B / Marginalia C / Broadsheet D / Plate E / Exposed Grid

Direction A

Ledger

The index is the homepage. There is no hero, no lede block, no "welcome" — you land inside the catalog of the work, with the standing facts stated above it as a header record. The bet: for someone whose product is written output, a marketing hero is a delay between the visitor and the thing they came for.

Trend it refuses: the oversized centered display headline as the mandatory first screen. Nearly every personal site in 2026 opens the same way. This one opens with rows.

Structural commitment
Catalog-first. Every page is a table with tabular figures and hairline rows; the current piece is a single ink-inverted row inside the run.
Type reassignment
Mono is promoted from dateline + code to the entire structural layer — column heads, dates, counts. Fraunces drops to row-title scale (19px) and never exceeds it.
Palette delta
None to the tokens. Inversion changes role: from feature block to row state (the live entry).
Kills
display-xxl, display-xl, the lede paragraph, the hero, the whole "featured" concept.
Risk
Cold on first contact. A first-time visitor gets no narrative framing — the standing-record header has to carry all of it in four lines.

Direction B

Marginalia

Navigation, dates, cross-references and asides all live in a fixed left margin rail set against the text column by a single vertical hairline. The body column is never interrupted — no inline chrome, no breadcrumb, no top nav bar. It reads like an annotated edition: the apparatus is beside the text, not wrapped around it.

Trend it refuses: the persistent top navigation bar, and the "sticky header that shrinks on scroll." Wayfinding is a margin note, which is where scholarly publishing has kept it for four hundred years.

Structural commitment
Two-track asymmetric page — 20ch rail + 1px spine + 62ch measure. The rail is right-aligned against the spine so both columns share one hard edge.
Type reassignment
A real drop initial on the H1 (Fraunces 500 at 1.5em, sunk into the line) replaces the olive type-accent as the emphasis device. Superscript mono markers key body text to rail notes.
Palette delta
Olive demoted to reference markers only — the superscripts and rail-note numbers. It never touches display type.
Kills
Top nav, the running-head/folio pair as separate primitives (running head absorbs both), inversion blocks entirely.
Risk
The rail is dead weight below ~900px and has to collapse to a stacked block — mobile loses the idea. Needs a real answer, not a media-query shrug.

Direction C

Broadsheet

Density as the position. Five pieces of content compete for attention on a single screen across three unequal columns divided by hairlines, the way a front page works. Nothing is centered, nothing is spaced out to breathe. The reader is trusted to scan.

Trend it refuses: whitespace-as-luxury. The dominant 2026 register signals seriousness by giving one idea the whole viewport. This one signals it by having enough to say that one idea can't have the whole viewport.

Structural commitment
Asymmetric three-column grid (1.55 / 1 / 0.82) with 1px column rules, a centered masthead, and a 2px strap rule. Stories are ranked by column width, not by card size.
Type reassignment
Fraunces 700 appears for the first time — masthead only. Display scale drops to 44px; the lede headline earns rank from column width instead of point size. Drop cap on the lead story.
Palette delta
Olive becomes the kicker color (the category line above a story) — a real recurring semantic role rather than one accented word.
Kills
display-xxl and display-xl outright. Generous spacing tokens (--s-9, --s-10) unused. Any section that occupies a full screen alone.
Risk
Density is one bad line-length away from noise, and it's the hardest of the five to hold as new content types arrive. Mobile is a full re-think, not a stack.

The split audience Writing for the human and the retrieval system at the same time

By Shane Logsdon · 2026.07.28 · 9 min

Every page you publish now has two readers, and they want opposite things. The human wants the argument to unfold. The retrieval system wants the answer stated flat, early, and attributably — and it will reward you for it whether or not the human ever sees the paragraph it lifted.

The reflex is to pick one and lose the other: write for the model and you produce answer-shaped mush no person finishes; write purely for the person and you're invisible to the thing that now decides what gets surfaced. The way through is structural, not stylistic.

What actually changed

Not the writing. The unit of retrieval. A model doesn't index your essay — it indexes the paragraph that answered the question, stripped of everything that made it yours.

Direction D

Plate

The v2 inversion becomes the page. Ink is the surface, cream is the type, and everything sits inside a single hairline frame with a hard margin around it — a printed plate rather than a screen. Olive is promoted to the only other color and does real work: labels, live states, the one word that matters.

Trend it refuses: dark mode as a toggle and as a dev-tool signifier. This isn't a theme — there's no light version of it. It also refuses the near-black-with-blue-glow register that every AI product ships; the surface is the same ink already in the system, and nothing glows.

Structural commitment
Full-bleed ink with a 26px margin and a 1px cream frame; every division inside is a frame line, not a gap. The page is a plate you look at, not a document you scroll through.
Type reassignment
Fraunces 300 at cover scale — the light optical cut reads correctly on dark where 400 blooms. Weight contrast inside a single headline (300 against 600) replaces the accent-word device.
Palette delta
The only real token change in the set: olive lifts to #a8c06a on ink. The v2 olive measures 3.2:1 against #0e1116 — large-text-only, and it fails AA for the 10–13px labels this direction puts it on; the lifted value measures 9.4:1. Cream/ink swap roles wholesale.
Kills
The cream page. --surface-muted and --surface-feature both become dead tokens. Hover-fill states (nothing to fill toward).
Risk
Long-form reading on dark is genuinely worse, and reading is the site's whole job. Honest version of this direction is dark shell + cream article pages, which is a compromise worth naming out loud.

Direction E

Exposed Grid

The 12-column grid stops being invisible scaffolding and becomes the visible surface. Column rules run the full height of the page; every element hard-aligns to a line; empty cells are left empty on purpose rather than filled with something. The structure is the ornament — there is no other ornament.

Trend it refuses: the soft-container look — a max-width column floating in undifferentiated space with rounded panels, where nothing tells you why anything is that wide. Here the width of every block is legible as a count of columns.

Structural commitment
Drawn 12-column field. Content spans stated column counts (1–7, 8–12) and never floats between rules. Deliberate voids: the hero occupies 7 of 12 and the remaining 5 stay blank.
Type reassignment
Everything flush-left to a grid line, no optical adjustment. Mono set vertically in a margin column as the running mark — the only rotated type in the system.
Palette delta
Ink fills cells, not bands: one entry per section is an ink-filled cell in the grid, so inversion becomes a positional device you can point at on the grid.
Kills
The centered container. Any element whose width isn't a column count. Soft section breaks — all divisions are grid lines.
Risk
Closest of the five to Swiss-revival, which has its own convergence problem. It stays distinct only if the voids stay genuinely empty; the first time a cell gets filled "because it looked bare," the direction is gone.
